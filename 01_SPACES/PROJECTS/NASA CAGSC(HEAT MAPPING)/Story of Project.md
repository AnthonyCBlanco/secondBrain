My team and I started ambitious. our original idea was to have a UAV that would use its GPS, temperature, humidity, and a thermal camera to map our urban heat islands on our campus. Unfortunately our materials came late and we were missing so critical supplies. I went and refactored our project to be a held held device with a thermal camera that we could position in way to collect temp readings over a large open area. Then came our next problem, we were not going to have a thermal camera in time to present our project. My next option was to keep with ambient readings of the temperature and humidity, and we were also missing a GPS sensor for our Arduino mega. Then came my our final project. 

Simply using the arduino to take ambient temperature readings and using my laptops on board gps to gather location data. The bypass the nightmare of windows location services. I built a simple web page that uses chromes location data to provide the needed context for out temp and humidity readings. The webpage also actively shows readings from the arduino as well
